# Northwind Labs — Information Security Policy

_Version 2.1 · Owner: Security Team_

## Passwords and authentication

- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
- Passwords must be rotated every 180 days, or immediately if a compromise is suspected.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN, source control and the HR portal.
- Hardware security keys are required for anyone with production access.

## Device security

All company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and the screen must lock after 5 minutes of inactivity.
Personal devices may access email only through the managed mobile app.

## Reporting incidents

Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@northwind.example within 1 hour of discovery.
Do not attempt to investigate a suspected breach yourself. Lost or stolen devices must be reported the same day.

## Data classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted.
Restricted data (customer payment data, health records) may never be stored on laptops or shared through chat tools.
