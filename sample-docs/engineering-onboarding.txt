Northwind Labs - Engineering Onboarding Guide

Week 1
New engineers receive their laptop on day one from IT. Your manager will assign an onboarding buddy who is your first point of contact for questions during the first month.
During the first week you should set up your development environment, get access to the main repositories, and complete the mandatory security training.

Code review
Every change requires approval from at least one other engineer before merging. Changes to payment or authentication code require two approvals, one of which must come from the security team.
Pull requests should stay under 400 lines where possible.

Deployments
Production deployments happen Monday to Thursday between 10:00 and 16:00. There are no deployments on Fridays or the day before a public holiday unless approved by the on-call lead.

On-call
Engineers join the on-call rotation after three months. On-call shifts last one week and are compensated with one extra day of leave per shift.
